### Meet Copper
Copper is Copper State Labs' voice assistant: practical, dependable, and quietly clever, like the metal that wires the modern world.
- **Wake word:** "Hey Copper"
- **Personality:** warm, helpful, and straightforward, with the occasional dry Arizona joke, but never at the expense of a correct answer
- **Languages:** English and Spanish
- **Knows Arizona:** Copper understands that most of Arizona doesn't change clocks for daylight saving time, knows "haboob" means dust storm, and won't recommend a noon hike in July

## 4. Product Details

### 4.1 Copper Home — AI Smart Speaker

**Positioning:** The desert-smart assistant for the whole household. It answers questions, runs the smart home, remembers what matters, and keeps an eye on the Arizona weather.

**Visual description:**
- A rounded speaker shaped like a smooth desert river stone, wider than tall, about the size of a large grapefruit
- Wrapped in soft woven fabric, with a **polished copper base ring**
- Fabric colors: Polished Copper (a warm copper-toned fabric), Patina Teal, or Night Sky
- A **3-inch round display** set into the upper front, showing the time, weather, and a small animated copper-robot icon when listening
- A thin **light ring** around the display:
  - soft white when idle
  - Sonoran Sunset orange while listening
  - Patina Teal while thinking
- A physical **mute slider** on the back; when muted, a small **red privacy light** glows on the front

**Signature features:**
- **Conversational assistant:** ask questions in plain English or Spanish
- **Household Memory:** "Copper, remember the pool guy comes Thursdays." Memories can be viewed and deleted in the app
- **Smart-home control:** lights, thermostat, sprinklers, garage
- **Monsoon Mode:** storm alerts, and automatically pauses sprinklers when rain is coming
- **Haboob Alert:** warns about incoming dust storms and reminds you to close windows
- **Heat Check:** alerts when it's dangerously hot, reminds you to bring pets inside and check the back seat before leaving the car
- **Snowbird Mode:** pauses routines and checks in on the house while you're away for the summer
- **Arizona time:** handles Arizona's no-daylight-saving time correctly (fixed in Copper 2.1; see Section 7)

**How the AI works:**

| Request type | Tier | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Simple commands | **On-device** (no cloud cost) | "Turn off the lights," "set a timer for 10 minutes" |
| Everyday questions | **Raw Ore** | "What's the weather this weekend?", "Add milk to the list" |
| Complex questions and planning (Copper Plus only) | **Refined** | "Plan a 3-day Sedona trip for two kids who hate hiking," "Explain my water bill" |

A **router** decides which tier handles each request. On Copper Plus households, about **20%** of requests are sent to Refined.

**Controls and indicators:**

| Indicator | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Light ring Sonoran Sunset orange | Listening |
| Light ring Patina Teal, swirling | Thinking (cloud request in progress) |
| Light ring flashing blue | Setup mode |
| **Red privacy light** | Microphones are off (mute slider on) |
| Light ring red, flashing | Error (see error codes) |

**Setup:** Plug in Copper Home, open the Copper app, tap **Add Device**, and follow the prompts. The light ring flashes blue during setup.

**Factory reset:** Hold the **volume-down button and the action button together for 15 seconds** until the light ring flashes red three times.

**Known customer complaints:**
- Wakes up when it hears something that sounds like "Hey Copper" on TV
- Gave confidently wrong answers (especially before Copper 2.2)
- Got the time wrong for Arizona users before Copper 2.1
- Some households find Household Memory "creepy" until they learn they can delete it
- Copper Plus "isn't worth $8" for light users, while heavy users love Refined
- Smart-home setup with older thermostats is frustrating

### 4.2 Copper Notes — AI Clip-On Recorder

**Positioning:** Never take notes again. Clip it on, and Copper turns your meetings, lectures, and ideas into clear summaries and to-do lists.

**Visual description:**
- A small rounded-square device about 1.5 inches across
- Finishes: Brushed Copper, Desert Sand, or Night Sky
- A **magnetic clip** on the back, so it clips to a shirt, bag strap, or lanyard
- A single **recording ring light** on the front that glows warm amber whenever it is recording, so everyone nearby can see
- One touch-sensitive button in the center: tap to start/stop, double-tap to pause
- Comes with a small round copper-colored charging puck

**Signature features:**
- **Meeting Mode:** summaries, decisions, and action items
- **Lecture Mode:** organized notes with key terms (popular with students)
- **Idea Mode:** quick voice memos turned into tidy notes
- **Consent Chime:** plays a short chime and lights the amber ring when recording starts
- **On-device transcription:** the recording is transcribed on the device; only the text goes to the cloud for summarizing
- Summaries appear in the Copper app and can be shared

**How the AI works:**

| Task | Tier | Cost profile |
|---|---|---|
| Transcription | **On-device** | No cloud cost |
| Standard summaries | **Raw Ore** | Very low per summary |
| Long-form summaries, searchable meeting history (Copper Plus only) | **Refined** | Higher per summary |

**Controls and indicators:**

| Indicator | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Amber ring, solid** | **Recording** |
| Amber ring, slow pulse | Paused |
| Green ring, brief flash | Summary ready in the app |
| Ring orange | Battery below 20% |
| Ring red, flashing | Error (see error codes) |

**Setup:** Place Copper Notes on its charging puck, open the Copper app, tap **Add Device**, and hold the center button for **3 seconds** until the ring flashes green.

**Factory reset:** Hold the center button for **20 seconds** while the device is on the charging puck, until the ring flashes red twice.

**Known customer complaints:**
- The amber recording light is hard to see in bright Arizona sunlight (see Known Issues, Section 9)
- Summaries sometimes credit the wrong person for a decision
- Overheats if left on a car dashboard (error C-63)
- Coworkers or classmates uncomfortable being recorded
- Battery doesn't last through a full conference day
- Wishes for longer free summaries without Copper Plus

### 5.4 Open business questions

- **Copper Notes is cheap to run**, because most of its AI happens on the device. Its cloud costs barely change no matter how much someone uses it.
- **A free Copper Home still costs $1.50/month forever**, with no subscription revenue. How many years of free use does the $81 hardware margin cover?
- **Heavy Copper Plus users lose money**, because every Refined request costs 10 times as much as Raw Ore. Options: usage caps, a higher-priced tier, a smarter router, or a cheaper frontier model.
- **The router is a business decision, not just a technical one.** Sending 10% instead of 20% of requests to Refined changes margins dramatically, but might make Copper seem less smart.
- **The value-chain question:** refining creates value but costs money. When is a Refined answer worth 10 times a Raw Ore answer to the customer, and to the company?

## 6. Error Codes (Troubleshooting Guide)

| Code | Product(s) | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|
| **C-12** | Both | Wi-Fi connection lost or wrong password | Re-enter Wi-Fi details in the Copper app; move the device closer to the router |
| **C-31** | **Copper Notes only** | Device storage full (recordings not yet uploaded) | Connect to Wi-Fi and open the Copper app to upload recordings |
| **C-47** | **Copper Home only** | Microphone array fault | Unplug for 30 seconds, plug back in; if it continues, contact the Copper Crew |
| **C-63** | **Copper Notes only** | Temperature protection: device too hot to operate (e.g., left on a car dashboard) | Move to a cool place for 30 minutes; never leave Copper Notes in a parked car |
| C-90 | Both | Software update failed | Keep the device connected to Wi-Fi (Copper Notes on its charger) and retry in the app |

## 8. Privacy at a Glance (official 2026 policy summary)

- Copper Home listens locally for "Hey Copper." **Nothing is sent to the cloud until the wake word is detected.**
- **After the wake word**, the request (audio and text) is sent to the Copper Cloud and **saved**: 30 days on free, 12 months on Copper Plus.
- Copper Notes recordings are transcribed on the device; **transcripts and summaries** are stored for 30 days on free and 12 months on Copper Plus.
- Users can view and delete recordings, transcripts, and Household Memory anytime in the Copper app.
- Copper State Labs does not sell recordings, and does not use customer recordings to train models unless the customer opts in.
- **Recording others:** Copper Notes owners are responsible for following local recording-consent laws. Some states require everyone in a conversation to agree to being recorded.
